Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Overview

WHOOP Daily SMS is a personal software project that retrieves health and activity information from the WHOOP API and sends selected information to the service owner's mobile phone via SMS.

Information Collected

The service accesses information provided by WHOOP through its API, including sleep, recovery, heart rate, strain, workout, activity, and related fitness metrics.

The service also uses a mobile phone number for the purpose of delivering SMS messages.

How Information Is Used

WHOOP data is used solely to generate the daily SMS messages provided by this service. The information is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or sale to third parties.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in/consent information are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS Messaging

The service sends recurring informational SMS messages containing WHOOP health and activity metrics.

Messages are generally sent twice per day, once in the morning and once in the evening. Message frequency may vary if the service is being tested, modified, or experiences an operational issue.

Message and data rates may apply.

Third-Party Services

The service uses WHOOP to provide health and activity data and Twilio to deliver SMS messages. Those services process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

API credentials and authentication tokens are stored using application configuration and local files and are not intentionally published in the source repository.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the service owner through the contact information associated with the service.
